# Chemical-exposomebase

**Project Overview**

This project involves the creation of a comprehensive exposome knowledge base that links genetic data, environmental toxicants, and population-specific allele frequencies. The goal is to provide a dynamic, user-friendly platform for exploring the intricate relationships between toxic chemicals, genetic variants, and their potential role in complex diseases. The project incorporates advanced bioinformatics and statistical techniques to enable the effective querying, collection, and visualisation of data through a Shiny interface, designed to facilitate intuitive exploration and analysis of the exposome.
